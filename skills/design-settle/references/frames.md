# The direction frames — Step 5, first half

**The frames run in every session, without exception**, after the install gate. **Nothing stands them down** — not a stressed reference (it gets its own frame), not a brand palette (a constraint every frame is drawn under, colour derived per `impeccable`), not Keep (today's look is one of the frames), not a user who seems to know what they want. **No value of the look is asked after them**: the picked frame is the direction, its values read at ratification, and what follows is judged as drawn (`canvas.md`, Judging).

## The material — loaded now

**Load `impeccable` and `frontend-design`, and `review-animations` where installed, now, in one turn** — after the install gate, before the first frame is composed. **Print `Material loaded:` naming every file read, by path, before the first frame** — a frame set shown without that line was drawn without the material. It stays in hand for the design plan, every round, the judgement and ratification; never re-read step by step, except at the escalation (`canvas.md`, Judging). Each is guidance, never an authority: where one collides with a user answer or a ratified value, the user wins.

**Never load a skill that names the look as material** — a fixed palette, pairing, card recipe, or shadow scale. Always load guidance that **narrows the space the look is chosen from** — the generated-looking defaults, the two-accents-one-family formula, the failed-choice typefaces. **`dataviz` is loaded for its method alone**: its palette is never used; the chart palette is `DESIGN.md`'s (`ratify.md`).

**What crosses from `impeccable` — named by role, located through its own `SKILL.md` index at load time, never by a remembered file name.**

- **Crosses:** its **craft floor** — the Refuse list, and the Verify list as a build-time check; its **two calibrations for new work** — the overused training-data display faces and the clusters AI interfaces converge on; its **operational register** entire **on an operational app**, never on a landing page. **The Refuse list crosses by what it forbids, not by the CSS it names**, so it holds on a Surface with no selectors.
- **Does not cross:** its **direction pipeline** (interview, dice roll, decision server, comps) and its **agents** — spawn none; `docs-format`'s list stays the only set of documents. **Never run an `impeccable` command that writes `PRODUCT.md` or `DESIGN.md` from the built world** (`init`, `document`, `extract` at the time of writing); answer its `init` offer by pointing at `DESIGN.md`. The one `DESIGN.md` is the one Step 6 writes. Report a role `impeccable` no longer carries at the Design material row; never invent it.
- **Two playbooks, routed by platform:** read its **audit** playbook (accessibility, performance, theming, responsiveness, implementation integrity, touch-target floors) or its **adapt** playbook (width and device-class rules) in the web or native variant matching the Surface, when a step needs one; its other playbooks stay out.
- **One collision, decided here:** where a ratified `DESIGN.md` line contradicts `impeccable`'s OKLCH colour derivation, `DESIGN.md` wins, as it does over every line in this skill.

**The detector reads HTML and CSS, so it runs only on web technology** — web UI, or a Tauri, Electron, or Capacitor shell. On a **truly native** Surface (SwiftUI, Compose, XAML and kin), skip every detector instruction in `design-settle` and report `n/a — native surface`, never a zero; those steps' checks come from the Surface's Proof profile.

- **A native Surface loads `impeccable`'s platform references in the detector's place**: its iOS and/or Android reference (both on an adaptive Surface) alongside the craft floor. Web technology loads neither.
- **A desktop Surface — shell or native — is researched, not referenced**: before the frames, one subagent pass (`SKILL.md`, What a run costs) searches the target OS's current design guidelines, vendor documentation first — navigation model, window chrome, control sizes, type ramp, materials, accessibility floors — and returns them with their sources, named in the Design material row. WebSearch unavailable → say so there, draw from model knowledge, and mark the platform rules unverified.
- **On macOS, `apple-design`'s pages are read first where installed** (`apple-design/SKILL.md` under `~/.claude/skills/` or the repo's `.claude/skills/`): the macOS ones and those for the controls the frames draw, located through its own lookup index, plus its cross-platform translation on a non-Apple stack — never its review mode, craft lens, or always-load set. The research covers what they leave open.

**What crosses from `frontend-design` depends on the Register — first visit or tenth use** — derived at `app-settle` question 1 and re-derived from the Roles whenever read.

- **Tenth use** (a tool people return to): take **the calibration**, **the two-pass method** (state the plan, critique it against the brief, say what changed), **the signature**, and **the restraint** (spend boldness once, then remove one thing); leave the marketing anatomy — **the aesthetic risk is the signature and nothing else**, never a posture that costs speed of use.
- **First visit** (landing page, marketing site): **the whole file crosses** — hero as thesis, landing anatomy as the brief's floor, a posture across the page allowed; the signature is still asked on its own, and calibration and restraint still bind.
- **Both registers** in one product: first-visit on public pages, tenth-use behind the login, per page group. `impeccable`'s floor applies whole on every branch.

**`review-animations`, where installed, binds motion in every round on a web-technology Surface, as `ui-build` defines it**: an animation it forbids is fixed, or stands at the judgement as a finding under its name — the standing of a Refuse-list hit. A native Surface takes motion from `impeccable`'s iOS or Android reference, or from the desktop research, instead.

**What crosses from `ui-ux-pro-max`.** Its generator and look searches name a look, so they enter only as frame ingredients, never as a rule a round is held to. Its UX and stack guidelines are the floor level with `impeccable`'s, as `ui-build` rules them: a don't they name is fixed in every round, or stands at the judgement as a finding under its name. Read its `design-system` references on token architecture and component states before the refactor after the pick; `ratify.md`'s five rules win over them. Its chart search picks a chart type only, never a colour.

## Composing the set — by Claude

**Compose 2–4 frames**, the count set by how much the answers leave open and stated in one line. **The set is never one frame.**

- **Every ticked direction is in the set**; a stressed reference has its own frame, drawn closely to it and measured against at the judgement — an unstressed tick costs nothing there.
- **The rest are Claude's own**, each from a source of a different kind than the ticks and each other — **a product from the search, or a thing from the subject's world** (an object, a place, a medium its users picture), since a set drawn from software alone inherits the nearest software's look.
- **Off the web, the platform's design language holds one frame** before Claude fills any.
- **Keep, where ticked, is a frame**: the running app at its real routes, never redrawn.
- **Only `Decide for me` ticked** → the whole set is Claude's, across the search's directions and the subject's world. Ticked beside others it adds nothing: the ticks are in the set and the rest are Claude's own, as above.

**Search `ui-ux-pro-max` once per frame, with that frame's own direction as the query, every frame's searches in one chained command** — its style, colour, and typography searches; its fonts search where a face needs replacing; its motion presets where a frame animates, for their timing only and never their library; its landing search only for a first-visit page group. A query built from the reading alone returns the same few results for every app of one kind. After a rejected set, and at the escalation, the searches re-run only with the steered query.

**Mix its results into any frame** — the generator's proposal, a style, a palette, a pairing — beside ingredients from the reference search or the subject's world. **Adjust each to this app**: move a stock value off its default, fix a pair that fails the floor, replace a face that lacks the app's script, and drop a result whose category misreads the app.

Four rules make the set a real choice:

- **Each candidate explores an axis that can be named** — *Warm editorial* against *Dense data-first*, never variant A against B, never five shades of one aesthetic.
- **Each candidate carries a motivation line and its main trade-off**, one line each; the motivation names every ingredient's source and its adjustment. The picked frame's source is what `DESIGN.md`'s Overview records as the reference.
- **Names are stable** for the rest of the session — never renumbered or renamed, including across a regeneration.
- **One is marked as the recommendation**, with a reason from this app.

Announce nothing before the pick beyond each frame's motivation and trade-off lines — no palette, pairing, or signature. The design plan runs after the pick (`canvas.md`).

## Drawing the frames

- **Two screens per frame: the proving page, and the second screen answered at the interview**, each in its populated state. Never the whole app, never posters. Hold **the proving page** through the flow: promotion moves it first and `build-flow` judges later pages against it.
- **Draw in the answered theme mode.** Only where the theme-mode answer was `Decide for me` may one frame use the other mode, where its direction only exists there, saying so in its motivation line; picking it settles the mode.
- **Full fidelity.** Type, colour, spacing, real copy, real fixture values — never grey boxes; bland is what this step catches. Each frame picks its own faces and loads them by link; the picked frame's pairing is the app's typography from then on, its package installed at ratification — where UI exists, in the pass's Foundations (`ratify.md`).
- **Drawn in the canvas's own format, in place, with the installed stack**: files under `src/design-canvas/` named `<page>.<candidate>.tsx` (the platform's own page format off the web), controls from the chosen library, viewed on the running route. No second format and no later swap exists anywhere in the flow.
- **A frame is a sketch in the final format; the production duties wait for the pick.** Until then each frame is **one file per screen and one stylesheet of its own**, and owes only what the user judges: full fidelity, real copy, realistic data whose numbers close, a candidate signature, tagged, the render scan below, `impeccable`'s floor, `ui-ux-pro-max`'s UX floor, `review-animations`' floor where installed, the accessibility floors, the platform rules. It owes nothing the user cannot see: no shared component file, no cross-frame control pattern, no contract-shaped fixtures file, no production token names or alias layer, no unshown state, no state hook. Values are raw in the frame's stylesheet and data inline — with Tailwind, raw arbitrary-value utilities, the stylesheet holding only font loading and keyframes.
- **Where *own components* was chosen, each frame draws its controls in its own anatomy**, or the directions differ by token only.

## The render scan — before any round is shown

**One subagent runs this scan at both widths and takes the round's captures** (`SKILL.md`, What a run costs), briefed with this file's path — this section and Capturing — the round's routes, and skill `build-flow`'s Section 5 browser rule; it fixes nothing, and the session fixes what it returns. Run `impeccable`'s detector against the round's running routes (its URL tier) and fix the defect class — clipped or overflowing containers and text, occlusion, broken images, script errors, an attribute the source writes (`aria-*`, `role`, `href`) missing or rewritten in the rendered page, an accessible name (a library's own included) not in the app's locale (`ui-build` names where missing library strings come from). **Act only on defects in a round**; a hit that judges the direction (nested containers, uniform spacing, a colour family) travels to Step 8. **Every capture of the round is read before the user sees it**, detector or not; fix clipping, overflow, occlusion, and labels that lie (a size label the frame does not show, an animated state a still renders as nothing). Never use this scan to re-open composition. Compute every number a frame prints as a claim, never type it.

**Name the detector command run and its hit count in the round's report**; where it did not run, write `detector not run` and why, never a clean scan.

## Capturing

- **Screenshot each round at the desktop width alone** — `DESIGN.md`'s desktop breakpoint (1440px where none exists yet, and in a redesign, which rebuilds it from zero — the old breakpoint returns only as a line of the gate's diff) — **and add the lowest supported width for a screen the render scan flags at it, in the refine round, and once the canvas verdict approves** (`canvas.md`, Judging), because every capture is an image some context reads. Capture in the browser skill `build-flow` Section 5 drives, each width set as the viewport. A page longer than the browser captures is captured in viewport-height segments. Where the Proof profile names something other than a browser, capture through its Visual line — same bounds or the platform's equivalent.
- **Shown inside its device.** Present the narrow capture inside a phone frame and the wide one inside a window frame. **No fake chrome inside the frame** — no painted status bar, keyboard, or fake tab strip carrying content. **The frame belongs to the entry route, never a page file.** With no capture tooling, report the run target and both widths; draw no frame around nothing.

## The pick

**One AskUserQuestion, single-select, asked with the frames on screen**: the running route, each frame's desktop screenshots, and **one side-by-side capture** from the entry route's compare page — every frame file in an iframe at the desktop width, the Keep candidate at its real route — handed over before the question.

- **Where Keep is a candidate, a subagent takes the Keep captures and the side-by-side and returns only their paths**; the drawing session hands the paths over without opening them or the compare page. No subagent available → the side-by-side shows the frames alone, and the user opens Keep's real routes themselves.
- **Read an Other answer carrying a correction** (*the second one, but with the brand green*) **or a mix** (*the layout of the first with the palette of the third*) as that frame with the change attached.
- **The question text says how to reject the set**: *none of them — type what is wrong under Other and I draw another set*. Reject-the-set deletes the set and redraws one fresh set to what was said wrong, under the same rules; a second rejected set opens the escalation (`canvas.md`, Judging). The pick is not a correction round.

**Picking another frame releases a stressed reference**: say so in one line; its point-by-point list never runs, and `DESIGN.md` records the picked frame's source alone. An escalation releases it too, unless the user stresses it again at the re-asked direction question.

**Picking `Keep — today's look`** ends the drawing: nothing is redrawn, the unchosen frames are deleted, and the flow continues at Step 6 — as fix-the-drift where `DESIGN.md` is written, as the ratification of measured values where none exists. **A Keep pick carrying a change** (*keep it, but with the brand green*) is not Keep: the refine round redraws the proving page to today's values, read from the styling files (`SKILL.md`, Blindness), with the change applied, and the flow is a redesign from there.

**One refine round, only when the pick carries a change.** A clean pick goes straight to the design plan. A correction or mix is redrawn once — both of the picked frame's screens with the change applied — shown at both widths and confirmed with one AskUserQuestion: use this · adjust it once more · back to the set. *Adjust it once more* runs once; then the plan follows whatever stands. It is not counted as a correction round. **The unchosen frames survive until this round closes**; then delete them, their screenshots, and the compare page.

Picked → `canvas.md`.
